# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SwiftGet to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **shubratodn44985@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently
ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.
